Precision from practice.

Since November 2026 I have been a working student in IT Touchpoint at Deutsche Börse Group, the personal point of contact for employees on everything related to their digital workplace. The focus is a user-centric service guided by the principle of putting people before technology. I help ensure that technology works seamlessly in everyday business, support the lifecycle management of mobile devices within a group-wide standardized, security-compliant device management framework, and contribute to planning and executing hardware rollouts. This gives me first-hand insight into how IT services are managed at a global financial market infrastructure provider.

My foundation comes from more than three and a half years at PCDOKTOR.de in Frankfurt, where I worked part-time (mini-job) alongside school, most recently as a Level 3 IT Support Engineer, the escalation level for cases first- and second-level support could not close: faults with no reproducible cause, systems that keep failing after standard remediation, and data loss where there is no second attempt. That included data recovery on damaged storage media, administering Windows and Linux systems, backup concepts with real restore tests, and removing malware together with root-cause analysis and system hardening.

Security is the part that interests me most: in 2025 I completed Cisco's Junior Cybersecurity Analyst Career Path along with Cyber Threat Management, Networking Basics and Digital Safety and Security Awareness. In my own homelab I rebuild networking and security projects so what I've learned gets tested on real hardware. In 2026 I earned my Fachhochschulreife (advanced technical college entrance qualification) with a focus on information technology at Hochtaunusschule Oberursel, and since October 2026 I have been studying Computer Science at TU Darmstadt. I sharpened my understanding of financial markets through job simulations by J.P. Morgan and Goldman Sachs: from M&A analysis and company valuation to financial modeling.
